# 👑 Elite App

**Elite App** is a social networking platform designed to help people connect, make new friends, and build meaningful connections worldwide. Discover and connect with people locally or internationally, follow interesting profiles, send friend requests, chat with friends, and enjoy a safe and engaging social experience.

* Followers & Following
* View User Profiles
* User Activity

### 🌍 Global Friend Discovery

* Connect with People Around the World
* Search & Explore User Profiles
* Make New Global Friends

### 🤝 Friends & Connections

### 🔎 Discover

* Discover Screen
* Find New Friends

* Discover People from Different Countries
* Connect with Like-minded People

## 🚀 Why Elite App?

Elite App makes it easy to meet new people and build friendships beyond geographical boundaries. Whether you want to connect with people nearby or make international friends, Elite provides a simple and engaging platform to discover people, build connections, follow profiles, send friend requests, and chat with friends.
